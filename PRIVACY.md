# Redthread Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 11, 2026

Redthread is a skills-only plugin. It does not operate an external server, create user accounts, or independently collect, store, sell, or share personal data.

The plugin processes photos and text that users intentionally provide through the host product. Working assets are kept within the active task workspace or temporary storage, and the workflow instructs the host to expose only the finished collage. Processing by ChatGPT, Codex, OpenAI, the operating system, or user-selected tools remains governed by those services' own terms and privacy policies.

Questions may be submitted through the repository support channel described in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md).
